# Recommended and compatible mods

Hospital works fine on its own. But a few mods slot in so nicely that your hospital starts
feeling like a whole new place.

Every mod here is optional. If it isn't installed, nothing changes.

All credit for these mods goes to their respective authors. Check out the pages of the ones linked
below and show them some love.

---

## Dedicated support

This fork supports these mods on purpose: it detects them, adapts patient generation and
treatment to them, and covers them with automated tests.

### Plasteel Surgery (Continued)

**Open an aesthetic medicine clinic, and then some.** Patients come in for ~~plastic~~ plasteel surgery
that raises their Beauty (or lowers it, if you really insist), for sex changes and body-type
changes. Fix up patients with Creepy breathing or an Annoying voice and give them a better
shot at love. And for the chronically sniffly: a shot of plasteel shavings straight into the
bloodstream to boost their immunity. What could possibly go wrong?

What's supported:
- Patients only ask for surgeries they can actually receive.
- Laryngoplasty patients arrive with Annoying voice or Creepy breathing.
- Beauty surgery patients arrive with a Beauty trait the surgery accepts.
- Boost immunity is never requested by Super-immune patients.
- Feminize is requested only by women, Masculinize only by men.
- Decrease Beauty and most body-type changes are blacklisted by default on new maps
  (you can turn them back on in the map settings).

Link: [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2018276375)

### MedPod

**Sci-fi medicine, hospital edition.** Lay patients in MedPods and let the machines do the
work, right down to regrowing missing limbs. Your doctors can finally have a coffee.

What's supported:
- Patients use MedPods like any other hospital bed.\*
- A condition cured by a MedPod counts as treated, and the patient pays for it\*, though for
  now only the minimum rate. Better MedPod treatment pricing is planned.
- A missing body part regrown by a MedPod counts too.

Link: [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2153065191)

### IV Drug Infuser

**Medicine on a drip.** Link a bed to an infuser and it doses the patient straight from its
tank, no doctor needed.

What's supported:
- Every dose the infuser gives a patient is added to the patient's bill: medicine at the
  price of a tend, other drugs at the price of an Administer bill.
- Hospital's patient drug policies count infused doses as given, so a scheduled drug is not
  administered again right after an infusion.
- The infuser gives a patient only the drugs their patient drug policies allow, at the
  policy's frequency. Medicine is always given. A drug the policies do not allow is not
  infused, and no fuel is used.
- Patients ignore infusers when choosing the best bed. This is on purpose: an infuser is
  free and instant to build, so either every bed already has one or the player can add one
  when needed. Taking infusers into account would complicate bed choice for an effect that
  would rarely be noticeable.

Support suggested by: Pasaway

### Multi-Doctor Surgery

**Real surgical teams.** Big operations deserve more than one pair of hands. Put together
a surgery team and let it handle your patients' operations.

What's supported:
- With Multi-Doctor Surgery's "(Beta) Fast Operation" option on and a default surgery team
  set, patient surgeries get that team.
- Otherwise a single doctor operates, as usual.

Link: [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3356249192)

\* Supported by the original Hospital too.

## Mods that fit well

No special support: these mods just pair nicely with running a hospital.

### Diseases Overhauled

**A much richer disease catalogue.** No two cases of flu are alike anymore: diseases get
random severity, can leave dormant pathogens that flare up years later, and can trigger
secondary illnesses and long-term conditions. On top of that come dozens of new diseases,
from toothache and appendicitis to tuberculosis and brain worms, plus new drugs to stock your
pharmacy: a strong painkiller, an immune booster and good old vegetable broth among them.

Hospital picks patient diseases from every tendable, life-threatening disease in the game,
so the new ones can turn up in your waiting room too.\*\*

Link: [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2174246491)

### Pharmacist: Represcribed

**Smarter use of your medicine cabinet.** Are your doctors wasting precious glitterworld
medicine on scratches from a social fight? Pharmacist can help.

Link: [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3527418098)

### Rejuvenate Surgery

**The fountain of youth, now in surgical form.** Nobody stays young forever... or do they?
All it takes is a bit of glitterworld medicine.

Link: [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1390755364)

### Smart Medicine - Continued

**Doctors who come prepared.** No more sprinting across the map for a single herbal
medicine while the patient bleeds out: doctors keep medicine in their pockets and can use
whatever the patient, other colonists or even a pack animal are carrying. They can reach for
cheaper medicine when it will do just as well, go easy on it for non-urgent wounds, and tend
patients right where they lie.

Link: [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3526251680)

### Vanilla Apparel Expanded

**Dress the part.** Put your doctors in scrubs and surgical masks and they'll finally look
like real medical staff. It's not just for show either: the outfit improves their treatment
results.

Link: [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1814987817)

### Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Medical Module

**Make it look like a real hospital.** A lot more choice of medical beds and the equipment
that links to them, plus sterile walls with color-coded stripes.

Link: [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1718191613)
