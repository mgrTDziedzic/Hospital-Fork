## 3.0.1
- Cosmetic UI changes: the "Hospital policies" window title uses the Medium font and an "Opening hours" label (new Keyed key `HospitalOpeningHours`, English 1.6 and 1.7) sits above the opening-hours selector; the window is 35 px taller. The "Hospital policies" button in the Hospital tab header has the size of the vanilla Assign tab policy buttons (32 px high, column width up to 360 px; `PawnColumnWorker_ButtonDisallow.DoHeader`, `GetMinHeaderHeight`).
- The patient table in the Hospital tab can be sorted by clicking a column header (vanilla behaviour: descending, ascending, off). Name, diagnosis and treatment are now sortable (`sortable` in `Defs/MainTab/PawnColumns.xml`, 1.6 and 1.7); the rating, time spent and bill columns sort by their numeric value instead of their formatted text (`Compare` overrides). Without a sort column the patients stay grouped by lord as before. The column workers share one cached `HospitalMapComponent` lookup (`MainTab/PatientTableHelper.cs`) that is also safe to use before anything was drawn.
- Patient backstory grammar (`RulePacks_PatientBackstory.xml`, 1.6 and 1.7) uses more vanilla word lists (animal groups, jobs, family, allies, enemies, natural objects, terrain features, mechanoids, communities, extra adjectives and concepts). The pack has its own word files with the `hosp_` prefix (business types and names, pet names, town names, curiosity adjectives, group names). New sentence shapes: cause chains ("was ... when ..."), bets and blame, more leave reactions, and lines that apply only to patients of tribal, outlander or pirate factions, royal title holders and faction leaders (constants `PATIENT_faction`, `PATIENT_royalInCurrentFaction`, `PATIENT_factionLeader`). Existing lines are kept.
- When a wounded patient with a backstory leaves, the leave message gets one more sentence that reuses the activity from the backstory (e.g. "Name went home, swearing never to do things like petting the hare again."). New grammar root `r_leave` in `RulePacks_PatientBackstory.xml`; `BackstoryUtility` resolves the activity first (own seed), then the backstory and the leave line with that activity injected as a high priority rule. `BackstoryUtility.GetLeaveLine` is empty without a backstory seed or when "Show patient backstories" is off. Texts are derived from the saved seed with their own Rand state, so existing backstory wording can differ from earlier 3.0.1 test builds. Dismissed patients and deaths get no extra line.
- New mod setting "Show patient backstories" (`Settings.ShowPatientBackstories`, default on, in the mod settings next to the arrival message option). When off, the backstory is hidden in the arrival letter and in the Diagnosis and Bio tab tooltips; the Bio tab chip stays. The backstory seed is still drawn at arrival either way, so random number use does not depend on a per-client setting (Multiplayer). New Keyed keys `ShowPatientBackstories` and `ShowPatientBackstoriesTooltip`.
- The Bio tab (character card) of an active patient shows a "Hospital patient" chip in the top element stack. Its tooltip lists the diagnosis and, for wounded patients, the generated backstory. Implemented as a Harmony transpiler on the private `CharacterCardUtility.DoTopStack` (`Patches/CharacterCardPatch.cs`) that adds the chip after the quest chips; if the vanilla method differs and the anchor call is not found, nothing is patched and one info line is logged. New Keyed key `HospitalPatientChip` (1.6 and 1.7 English).
- Generated backstories for patients with a disease (e.g. "Name caught flu while kissing the hare."). New `RulePackDef Hospital_DiseaseBackstory` (`Defs/RulePackDefs/RulePacks_PatientDiseaseBackstory.xml`, 1.6 and 1.7) with the same roots as the wound pack (`r_backstory`, `r_leave`, `activity`), cause chains, bets and blame, faction and title lines, and leave reactions. `[DISEASE_label]` is the label of the patient's disease hediff (`GrammarUtility.RulesForDef("DISEASE", def)`), so modded diseases are named automatically; if the def no longer exists a generic label is used. The activity pool follows the constant `DISEASE_category` (new enum `DiseaseCategory`: Respiratory, Insect, Parasite, Mechanites, Infection, Toxic, Generic), chosen from the disease defName by `BackstoryUtility.CategoryOfDisease` (vanilla names, then name fragments such as "infection", "lung", "mechanite"; unknown or modded diseases use Generic). `PatientData` saves the new field `BackstoryDisease` (disease defName; null for wound backstories); the category is stored in the existing `BackstoryWound` field, so older saves load unchanged. `DiseaseUtility.AddRandomDisease` and `AddPlague` record the defName. `IncidentWorker_PatientArrives` calls the new `BackstoryUtility.AssignBackstory`, which uses the patient's final type (a disease patient that fell back to wounds gets a wound backstory; surgery patients get none). Display points (arrival letter, Diagnosis column tooltip, Bio tab chip, leave message) and the setting "Show patient backstories" cover both kinds; the Keyed tooltip text now says "wounded and sick patients". Mass casualty event patients get no backstory, as before. New `HospitalDefOf.Hospital_DiseaseBackstory`.
- I added a generated backstory for wounded patients (e.g. "Name suffered severe injuries while petting the hare."). It is added to the arrival letter (and the arrival message when that setting is on) and shown as a tooltip on the Diagnosis column of the Hospital tab. The activity matches the dominant wound kind (gunshot, burn, blunt, sharp), with a shared pool of unrelated activities for any wound. Texts come from the vanilla grammar engine: new `RulePackDef Hospital_WoundBackstory` (`Defs/RulePackDefs/RulePacks_PatientBackstory.xml`, 1.6 and 1.7), new `Utilities/BackstoryUtility.cs` (wound classification from the pawn's injuries, seeded text resolution with its own Rand state, so the game's random sequence is not touched). `PatientData` saves `BackstorySeed` and `BackstoryWound`; saves without them show no backstory. Disease, surgery and mass casualty patients have none. New `HospitalDefOf.Hospital_WoundBackstory`; `PawnColumnWorker_Diagnosis` overrides `GetTip`; `IncidentWorker_PatientArrives.TryExecute` assigns the backstory.
- Patients arrive while visitors from factions hostile to each other are on the map; only pawns that are not guests of the colony are checked for hostility between factions (`IncidentHelper.GetPotentialPatientFactions`). Pawns hostile to the player still block arrivals.
- Optional compatibility with IV Drug Infuser (`akaster.ivdruginfuser`, `Compat/IVDrugInfuserCompat.cs`, key added to `Compat/SupportedMods.cs`): Doses given by IV Drug Infuser to patients are added to the patient's bill; Hospital's patient drug policies count them as given. Hospital patches `IVDrugInfuser.Helper.Infused(Thing, Pawn)` by reflection (Prefix keeps the thing's def, Postfix bills active patients: medicine like the tend bill, `min(1.2 x market value, 70)`; other drugs like an Administer bill, `max(market value, 10)`; and sets `LastDrugTick`). One info log line when the mod is detected; nothing when it is absent.
- IV Drug Infuser gives patients only the drugs their patient drug policies allow, at the policy's frequency; medicine is always given. Each dose is added to the patient's bill and counts as given for the policy. Hospital patches the private `IVDrugInfuser.CompMultiRefuelable.TakeDrug()` (Prefix, by reflection) and skips the dose before fuel is consumed when no applicable policy list allows the drug now (`DrugUtility.PolicyAllowsNow`; healed patients get no drugs, like Administer bills); the infuser retries on its next check. An Administer bill still waiting for a drug the infuser just gave is removed. Dev mode logs one line per withheld drug. If the infuser API differs, only this limiting is turned off.
- Dev mode: each charge added to a patient's bill is written to the log with its reason. The base fee on arrival, the payment at discharge and the bill reset after a failed surgery get one line each (`PatientUtility.LogBill`; the `AddToBill` and `AddSurgeryBill` methods take an optional `reason`).

## 2026-11-16
- New: patient drug policies. In Hospital Policies pick a vanilla drug policy for each of five lists: "All patients", "Infection" (immunizable disease not yet immune), "Surgery" (real surgery bill or pending cure), "Injuries" (non-permanent injury or blood loss) and "Scars" (permanent injury). A patient gets the union of every list that applies. Drugs with "Scheduled" ticked in the policy are administered by doctors as "Administer X" medical bills placed at the top of the patient's bill list (checked every 250 ticks). The entry's frequency (counted from the last completed dose, not from a failed attempt), mood limit and joy limit are respected, there is at most one waiting bill per drug, and nothing is added for a patient who would be sent home anyway (same rule as the discharge check, so a scar or blood loss alone never keeps a patient, while a tended but still sick or healing patient keeps getting drugs). Each list has a "Teetotalers too" toggle (defaults: All off, Infection on, Surgery on, Injuries on, Scars off); teetotaler patients skip lists whose toggle is off. Leftover drug bills are removed when the patient leaves. Drugs are billed like any other medical bill. Settings are saved per map and synced in Multiplayer.
- Fix: completing or deleting a medical bill other than the cure surgery (e.g. an administered drug) no longer clears the patient's pending-surgery flag; only the cure recipe does.
- Fix: unfinished "Administer drug" bills no longer keep a patient in the hospital or send them to a surgery bed (new `SurgeryUtility.HasRealSurgeryBills`). Vanilla `ShouldSeekMedicalRest` counts any bill that should be done now, so the discharge check now uses `SurgeryUtility.ShouldSeekMedicalRestIgnoringBills` instead.

Beds marked for surgery are still preferred by surgery patients and avoided by the others; on equal stats the nearer bed wins.
